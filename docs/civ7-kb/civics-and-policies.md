## Specialist decisions are local

**OFFICIAL S2:** specialist base yields/maintenance changed in Test of Time. Specialists on current-Age or Unique Ageless buildings can be much more expensive than those on obsolete/warehouse infrastructure. The campaign's perks, policies, terrain and adjacencies further changed the net preview.

**OBSERVED Copan T33:**

- Fourth specialist in the old Observatory district: +3 science/+1 culture, about -0.8 food/-1.6 happiness.
- Schoolhouse/Lab district: +5 science/+1 culture, about -4.8 food/-5.6 happiness.

The cheaper old-district specialist was recommended because a Rail Station was underway and happiness was constrained. Higher nominal science was not automatically better. Later Social Science and additional changes altered these numbers.

At T50, Copan had 7 specialists and Bàakʼ 4: **11 x 3 = 33 base science** was the starting estimate for Social Science, not a proven final empire-wide delta. Applicable city multipliers and secondary effects can change net results. Local happiness was only about +10.4/+13.1 before that decision. By T51 Copan was about +4.8.

## Use the policy UI preview

The user compared **Living Standards +11 gold/+8 happiness** against **Bourgeoisie +12 culture/+12 gold** earlier in the run. With comfortable happiness, Bourgeoisie was preferred. Later upkeep increased and both were equipped as capacity expanded.

Do not compare base text in isolation when an actual swap preview is available. The displayed delta already accounts for removing the old card; do not add its contribution twice. Check all three cities' happiness, not only the empire total. Slotting both Humanism and Social Science to chase culture/science can be worse than one well-supported card.

## Government versus ideology

**OBSERVED:** Elective Republic was selected T2. Its specialist passive gave +1 science/+1 culture with +1 happiness upkeep per specialist. Its science Celebration choice was a **lump science award**, described as 1,000 scaled by game speed—not the obsolete "+20% science for six turns" description previously recalled. Definition amounts are not final runtime quotes.

Government and ideology are separate systems. A native government operation check returning success after adoption did not prove the chooser was available; the repository now separates pending selection from operation permission.

At T44, ideology branches exposed public descriptions, but entry research was blocked. Political Theory explicitly unlocked ideology trees later. Do not promise first-adopter slots from public conditional definitions: adoption order, eligibility and diplomacy were not established.

**Observed public branch ideas, not endorsed choices:** Communism included specialist science, scientific-attribute/town science and later project production; Democracy included specialist happiness/culture and later Wonder production. These might matter after unlocking, but their opportunity costs and rival relationships need live comparison. The player was not seeking a diplomatic/cultural victory pivot.
