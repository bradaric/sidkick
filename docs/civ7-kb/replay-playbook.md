## Choose the kind of retry deliberately

- **Modern T1 rescue:** preserves the story and much good development, but also Hatshepsut's inherited Tourism. Needs faster scoring/containment, not just a polished copy of the old queues.
- **Earlier Exploration replay:** more time to deny Wonders, collect scoring assets, compete for Natural Wonders/relics and enter Modern closer to the leader.
- **Fresh campaign:** more room to shape the whole victory race, but more replay time. Not a necessary conclusion from one loss.

A peaceful Modern win is **not guaranteed**. Culture is not proved universally superior, and war is not proved mandatory. If the loaded T1 position has no plausible peaceful defense before the scheduled threshold, say that before recommending fifty turns of investment.

## Peaceful branch: score earlier, not just culture faster

1. Keep the intended science core, but identify the shortest usable Innovation path and the minimum production city infrastructure. Do not modernize every yield in every city by habit.
2. Bring Natural History forward **with** a recruitment/research/display plan. Buying one timely Explorer can preserve core production; buying one without an available University/Museum or reachable opportunity does not solve the pipeline.
3. Take Natural History II when reachable Natural Wonders give useful personal finds before the deadline. Verify the switch, arrival, reward acknowledgement and display.
4. Compare targeted rail connections and different Unique Improvement types. Avoid constructing many stations whose full network cannot arrive in time.
5. Compare Flight II and Aerodromes in **retained Unique Building** cities. Count only assets that survive placement; retain the expected +6 per Maya-building city as a hypothesis until verified in the ledger.
6. Choose scoring Wonders if affordable in time and enjoyable. Oxford was a success; Hermitage was fun and awarded +12, but neither is automatically part of an optimal rescue order.
7. Preserve happiness for specialists. Free Speech/Constitution, Computation, targeted happiness buildings and resource assignments can support science; they are not direct Tourism defense.

Do not advertise a culture-yield card as a solution to a Tourism-point deficit. Extra civic speed helps only if it unlocks a scoring action that can finish in time. A culture-to-science leader ability may still make culture valuable; use the actual effect.

## More aggressive branch: decide early enough for it to matter

First clarify whether the user means **tempo/denial** or **war**. A focused science game can compete more aggressively for artifacts, Wonders and resources without conquest.

If the user wants military containment, assess:

- Visible/revealed access to the relevant rival settlements, not imagined undefended targets.
- Own force health, promotions, commander logistics, movement, siege capability, reinforcements and treasury.
- Native diplomatic eligibility, war support, happiness, trade loss and likely strain on our science plan.
- Which captured assets could produce our Tourism or remove current scoring assets, and what historical points may remain. Do not assume all score transfers or disappears.
- Whether a limited objective can be achieved **before** the rival deadline. A declaration on the last countdown turn is not a verified stop button.

Ask the user before changing the campaign's peaceful premise. If the required war cannot plausibly be prepared from Modern T1, an earlier replay may be more honest than promising a surgical rescue.

## Emergency and stopping rules

When a countdown starts, use [the triage protocol](victories-and-deadlines.md). Do not spend gold on a small contribution without a credible combined path to the required total. Do not promise the user a guaranteed rescue, or insist on war when they prefer concession.

If the user chooses to let the campaign go, respect it. Record the actual observed endpoint, preserve lessons and stop inventing last-minute orders. A concession is not proof a defeat screen was seen; a promising alternative is not a tested win.
